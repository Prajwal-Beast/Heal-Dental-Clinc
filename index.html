Our Services

Treatments designed
around you.

From routine cleaning to full-mouth rehabilitation — every treatment is planned with you, not just for you.

/ 01

General Dentistry

Your oral health foundation. We perform comprehensive check-ups, professional scaling & polishing, digital X-rays and cavity fillings — all in one visit. Twice-yearly visits prevent 90% of major dental problems before they start.

30 – 60 MIN From Rs. 1,200
/ 02

Teeth Whitening

Professional in-clinic laser whitening removes years of staining from coffee, tea and food — lifting teeth up to 8 shades in a single session. Safe for enamel, long-lasting results and dramatically brighter smile, same day.

60 – 90 MIN From Rs. 2,500
/ 03

Cosmetic Dentistry

Transform your smile with ultra-thin porcelain veneers, composite bonding and precision edge contouring. Each case is individually designed — we show you a digital preview before touching a single tooth. Confidence you can see immediately.

90 – 120 MIN From Rs. 4,800
/ 04

Orthodontics

Straighter teeth at any age — with discreet clear aligners or traditional braces. We treat crowding, spacing, overbites and underbites. Treatment plans are fully customised with progress tracked every 6 weeks until your perfect result.

Free Consultation From Rs. 3,500
/ 05

Dental Implants

The gold standard for missing teeth. Titanium posts are placed directly into the jawbone, topped with a ceramic crown that looks, feels and bites exactly like your natural tooth. Implants preserve jaw bone and can last a lifetime with proper care.

2 – 3 HRS From Rs. 18,000
/ 06

Root Canal Treatment

Modern root canal therapy is nothing to fear. Using rotary instruments and local anaesthesia, we remove infected pulp and seal the canal painlessly — saving your natural tooth, stopping the pain and preventing spread of infection.

60 – 90 MIN From Rs. 3,200
/ 07

Crowns & Bridges

Restore cracked, broken or missing teeth with precision-milled ceramic or zirconia crowns. Dental bridges fill gaps seamlessly using neighbouring teeth for support. All restorations are colour-matched to your natural teeth for an invisible finish.

2 sessions From Rs. 5,500
/ 08

Gum Disease Treatment

Bleeding gums and bad breath are early signs of gum disease. Our periodontal team performs deep scaling, root planing and antibacterial irrigation to eliminate infection, stop bone loss and restore healthy pink gums — before it becomes irreversible.

45 – 90 MIN From Rs. 2,000
/ 09

Pediatric Dentistry

Child-friendly care that makes dental visits something kids actually look forward to. We provide first teeth check-ups, sealants, fluoride treatments and early orthodontic screening for children from age 3. Building good habits from the very first tooth.

30 – 45 MIN From Rs. 800

About Heal

A modern clinic in
the heart of Kathmandu.

Founded on the belief that dental care should be accessible, comfortable and exceptional.

Located on Tokha Road, opposite Kumari Bank, Heal Dental Clinic combines modern clinical technology with genuine personal care. Every visit is planned around you — open every day, 8 AM to 8 PM.

  • State-of-the-art equipment

    Digital OPG X-rays, rotary endodontics, laser units and CAD/CAM crown milling — technology that means faster treatment and less discomfort.

  • Open every day, 8 AM – 8 PM

    Weekends, public holidays, no exceptions. Emergency slots are held every day. If you’re in pain, we will see you the same day.

  • Transparent, written pricing

    Every estimate is in writing before any treatment starts. No hidden charges, no pressure. You decide when you’re ready.

  • Specialist team under one roof

    Implantologist, orthodontist and pediatric dentist on-site. Comprehensive care for every member of the family, every day of the year.

  • 5.0 Google Rating — 8+ years running

    Hundreds of verified five-star reviews from patients across Kathmandu. Not a fluke — a consistent standard of care built over nearly a decade.

Our Team

The people behind
your smile.

Experienced, compassionate dentists who listen before they treat.

Lead Dentist
Dr. Priya Sharma
BDS, MDS — Implantology

With over 8 years specialising in implant dentistry and full-mouth rehabilitation, Dr. Sharma brings international training standards to every procedure. She graduated from Kathmandu Medical College and completed her MDS at BP Koirala Institute. Patients describe her as patient, reassuring and exceptionally skilled — especially for complex cases.

Orthodontist
Dr. Rohan Thapa
BDS, MDS — Orthodontics

Dr. Thapa is Kathmandu’s leading clear-aligner specialist, treating teens and adults with both Invisalign-style aligners and precision metal braces. Known for meticulous treatment planning and attention to bite function — not just aesthetics. He has successfully corrected over 400 orthodontic cases since joining Heal in 2018.

Pediatric Care
Dr. Anisha Karki
BDS — Pediatric & General

Dr. Karki has built a reputation as the dentist children in Tokha actually want to see. Her calm, playful approach eliminates dental anxiety from a child’s very first visit. She provides first-tooth check-ups, sealants, fluoride treatments and early orthodontic screening — setting young patients up for a lifetime of healthy teeth.
