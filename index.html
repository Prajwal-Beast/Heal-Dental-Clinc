Our Promise

Dentistry that
honours you.

At Heal Dental Clinic, exceptional care is built on trust, transparency, and genuine compassion. Every visit begins with listening — understanding your concerns before we ever reach for a tool. We are a neighbourhood clinic with one unbreakable promise: every patient is seen by a senior clinician, every time.

All treatment plans explained in plain language before we proceed — no surprises, ever.

Pain-minimal protocols — modern anaesthesia, rotary endodontics, and gentle technique.

Walk-ins welcome. Family-friendly environment. Open every day from 8 AM.

What We Offer

Comprehensive Dental Care

i.

Cleaning & Scaling

Ultrasonic scaling and polish to eliminate tartar, plaque, and stains. Recommended every six months for optimal periodontal health.

Preventive · 30–45 min
ii.

Root Canal

Single-sitting RCT with rotary endodontics. Save the natural tooth, end the pain — typically resolved in one painless visit.

Restorative · 1–2 visits
iii.

Dental Implants

Titanium implants with ceramic crowns. Permanent, natural-looking replacement with a three-month osseointegration protocol.

Surgical · 3 visits
iv.

Braces & Aligners

Metal, ceramic and clear aligner systems. Quarterly reviews with our orthodontic consultant for precision, lasting results.

Orthodontic · 9–24 months
v.

Teeth Whitening

In-chair laser whitening and custom take-home trays. Up to six shades brighter in a single session, entirely enamel-safe.

Cosmetic · 1 visit
vi.

Children’s Dentistry

Calm, welcoming first visits. Sealants, fluoride treatments, and habit counselling designed for children from age three.

Pediatric · 30 min
