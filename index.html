Restorative

Dental Implants

The gold standard for replacing a missing tooth. A medical-grade titanium post is placed directly into the jawbone and topped with a custom ceramic crown — looking, feeling and biting exactly like a natural tooth. Implants preserve jawbone structure, prevent neighbouring teeth from shifting and can last a lifetime with proper care.

  • Bone density assessment & CT scan planning
  • Titanium implant post placement
  • Healing cap & osseointegration period
  • Custom ceramic crown fabrication
  • Colour & shape matched to natural teeth
  • Long-term aftercare programme
2 – 3 HRS From Rs. 18,000 Book Now →

Endodontics

Root Canal Treatment

Modern root canal therapy is nothing like its reputation. With local anaesthesia and precision rotary instruments, we gently remove infected or damaged pulp, clean and shape the canals, and seal the tooth permanently — all while you remain completely comfortable. The tooth is saved, the pain is gone.

  • Pulp infection diagnosis & X-ray
  • Local anaesthesia for full comfort
  • Rotary instrumentation of root canals
  • Antimicrobial irrigation & disinfection
  • Biocompatible canal sealing
  • Crown recommendation to protect the tooth
60 – 90 MIN From Rs. 3,200 Book Now →

Crowns & Bridges

When a tooth is cracked, broken, severely decayed or missing, a crown or bridge restores full function and appearance. Our restorations are precision-milled from zirconia or ceramic — individually colour-matched so they blend seamlessly with your natural teeth. No one will know the difference.

  • Tooth preparation & digital impressions
  • Temporary crown while you wait
  • Precision zirconia or ceramic milling
  • Individual colour matching
  • Bite adjustment & occlusion check
  • Permanent cementation & polish

/ 08
Periodontics

Gum Disease Treatment

Bleeding gums and persistent bad breath are early warning signs of periodontal disease — the leading cause of adult tooth loss. Caught and treated early, gum disease is completely reversible. Our periodontal team uses deep scaling, root planing and antibacterial irrigation to stop bone loss and restore healthy gums.

  • Periodontal pocket depth measurement
  • Professional subgingival scaling
  • Root surface planing & debridement
  • Antibacterial pocket irrigation
  • Laser-assisted gum therapy (where needed)
  • Ongoing maintenance & recall programme
45 – 90 MIN From Rs. 2,000 Book Now →
